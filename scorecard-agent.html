Site Quality Scorecard Agent

The monthly quality scorecard global pharma companies use for site reviews and ICH Q10 management review. Enter the monthly counts; the agent calculates each KPI, rates it Green, Amber or Red against your targets, shows the 12-month trend and drafts the management review summary. Includes the FDA quality metrics (lot acceptance rate, invalidated OOS rate, product quality complaint rate). Sample data for 12 months is pre-filled (fictitious).

Targets (Green at or better, Amber within the margin, Red beyond)

KPI definitions follow common industry practice and FDA's draft quality metrics guidance; align them with your company's own definitions. Planning aid only; nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
