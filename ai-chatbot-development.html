How a project works

  1. Review your website, customer questions and support process.
  2. Plan conversation flows and the required integrations.
  3. Build and test an approved solution, then document how your team can manage it.

Final scope, integrations, delivery and pricing are agreed after reviewing your requirements. Demos on this site are prototypes, not live customer integrations.

Example business use cases

Service enquiries: Answer approved questions about services, operating hours and locations. Lead qualification: Ask a small set of agreed questions and route promising enquiries to a team member. Customer handoff: Provide a clear path to a real person when the answer requires judgment.

To scope a chatbot, we typically need your approved FAQs, services, contact channels, escalation rules and an example of the enquiries you receive.
