PARTNER & CUSTOMER PROTECTION

Verified partners. Clear payment rules.

Our anti-fraud policy applies to every local and international sales representative. An application alone does not make someone an authorised AAROHAN AI WORLD partner.

Verify before trusting

🪪 Verify before approval

We independently review the applicant's identity, company or agency registration where applicable, professional presence, business references and sales experience. Approval and a unique partner ID are provided only after a written agreement.

💳 Pay only the company

Customers must pay only against a company-issued invoice through a payment method independently confirmed with AAROHAN AI WORLD. Partners cannot collect deposits into personal accounts, wallets, UPI IDs, crypto wallets or cash on our behalf.

🛡️ No joining fees or guarantees

We do not charge applicants any registration or 'activation' fee. No partner may promise guaranteed earnings, false testimonials, unlimited features, fixed delivery dates or unapproved prices.

📝 Commission only after checks

Commissions are calculated under a signed agreement, matched to a unique approved deal and eligible cleared company receipts, checked for duplicate claims, refunds and costs, and approved by the company before any payout.

⚠️ Verify a salesperson or report suspected fraud: Check that their identity, partner ID, proposal and invoice are approved by us before paying or sharing confidential information. Never share OTPs, passwords, account access or unnecessary identity/banking documents in this form. Verify / report via official WhatsApp ↗

This is a policy and verification request process, not an automated identity check or payment-monitoring system. Cross-border payments, tax documents and payouts require further verification and approved providers.

Illustrative agreement principles

  1. No fixed salary; work independently as an external partner.
  2. 25% on eligible new project receipts and 10% on eligible renewals are proposed rates only.
  3. Tax, payment gateway fees, refunds, discounts and third-party costs are treated as agreed in writing.
  4. Partners must not promise integrations, go-live dates, fixed prices or capabilities not approved by the company.
  5. Customer data, consent, confidentiality and lawful marketing must be respected.
  6. Commission due dates, territory, conflicts, renewals and termination will be stated in a signed agreement.
  7. Partners must complete independent verification, sign a written agreement and use only company-approved sales materials and invoices.
  8. Never collect client payments or onboarding fees personally; potential fraud or conflicting leads must be reported promptly.
