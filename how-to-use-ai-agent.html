How to use an AI agent

Hindi voice with English subtitles. 65 seconds. हिंदी आवाज और English subtitles.

Steps shown in the video
  1. Open the AI Agent Studio page and pick an agent.
  2. Fill the short form: purpose, who it is for, key points.
  3. Choose tone, where you will send it and the answer language.
  4. Tap Create my prompt.
  5. Tap Open in ChatGPT or Open in Claude, or copy it into Gemini.
