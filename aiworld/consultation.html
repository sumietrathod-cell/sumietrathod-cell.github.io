COMMON QUESTIONS

About your free consultation

Does selecting a time reserve the meeting?

No. This is a preferred time request, not a live availability calendar. We confirm an appointment personally after checking availability.

Can international clients book across time zones?

Yes. Choose your timezone and the form converts your requested time to India time, including seasonal clock changes where applicable.

Is the first conversation really free?

The introductory 15-minute discussion is free. Software implementation, hosting, integration work and ongoing support are quoted separately.

Can I share my screen?

Yes, via the external Jitsi Meet service on compatible desktop browsers or supported mobile apps. Video meetings are arranged after confirmation.
