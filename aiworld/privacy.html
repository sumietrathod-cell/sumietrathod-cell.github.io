Privacy & data notice

Information about this public demonstration site; it is not legal advice.

For this version of the website: Our own website scripts do not collect form submissions on a server, set tracking cookies, or use a web analytics provider. GitHub Pages, which hosts this site, may process technical request information under its own policies. The sample dashboards run locally in your browser with fictional data. The optional LeadFlow demo stores lead entries in your browser’s local storage so they remain available on that device; it does not send those entries to our servers. You can clear its entries using the Clear all button or by clearing browser site data. The ReportPilot CSV demo processes files locally in the page without uploading them.

When you contact us

Our inquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message. When you choose to open WhatsApp and send it, your message is handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy practices and is delivered to our business contact. We do not receive anything merely because you typed it in the form.

Information you voluntarily provide

We may receive your name, company, email, phone number and project details when you contact us. We would use those details to respond to your inquiry, discuss scope and provide a quotation. Do not send patient data, employee records, regulated QA datasets or confidential documents without a separate agreed secure process.

Your choices

You can choose not to send the prepared message. For data-related requests, contact us through the business channel listed below. This notice will be updated if analytics, cloud CRM, lead capture, cookies or international data transfer mechanisms are introduced.
