FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Before your call

Do I need to install an app?

Jitsi Meet can open in supported browsers. Some mobile devices may offer its app. Please allow camera and microphone access when prompted.

Will the call start immediately?

Only if our representative is available and confirms the request. The meeting link is generated immediately, but sending a WhatsApp message and host participation are required.

Is my meeting room shared publicly?

Your room uses a random, high-entropy link that is not posted on this website. Share it only with people you trust. Jitsi provides the video service and has its own privacy terms.

What if nobody is available?

Use “Schedule a Video Call for Later” to send your preferred time and timezone. We can agree on a call time through WhatsApp.
