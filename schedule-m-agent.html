Schedule M Gap Assessment Agent

Check your site against the key requirements of revised Schedule M (Good Manufacturing Practices and requirements of premises, plant and equipment for pharmaceutical products, notified December 2023). Mark each point as Yes, Partly, No or Not applicable. Critical points count double. You get an area-wise score, critical gaps first and a prioritised action plan. A sample assessment is pre-filled.

Based on the key requirements of revised Schedule M (G.S.R. 922(E)). It is a self-assessment aid, not legal advice or a regulatory inspection. Check the official notification and dosage-form specific parts for your products. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
