What is Aarohan QA Analytics?

Aarohan QA Analytics is a portfolio and reporting practice by Sumit Rathod, a Pharma Quality Assurance professional with data analytics training in MS Excel, Power BI, SQL and PowerPoint.

Do you build Pharma QA dashboards in Excel and Power BI?

Yes. The portfolio shows sample dashboards for deviation, CAPA, OOS/OOT, APQR, stability, calibration, cleanroom environmental monitoring and cold chain excursions. Scope is agreed project by project.

Do these dashboards replace validated GxP systems?

No. Demonstration dashboards do not replace validated GxP systems, SOPs, QA approvals or regulatory decision-making.

How do I start a reporting or dashboard project?

Message on WhatsApp or send an email using the contact buttons below. Share what you want to measure, automate or visualize, and we will agree the scope.

Where is my data stored when I use an agent?

In your own browser, on your device. Agents do not send your entries to Aarohan, and we cannot see them. Use the Download Excel backup button to keep a copy on your computer.

How does the license key work?

After payment you receive a personal key and a one-tap activation link. The key is digitally signed with your name, plan and expiry date, so it cannot be forged. It unlocks only the agents in your plan.

Can I share my key with another company?

No. A license is for one person or, with a Company license, one company. Sharing or reselling is not allowed and a misused key can be deactivated.

Do I own the agent after buying?

You buy a license to use it, like Microsoft Office or Tally. Aarohan keeps the master copy so bug fixes and new features reach you automatically. An offline own-company edition is available on request.

What is the offline Excel edition?

A password-protected Excel tool with your company name that works without internet. Formulas are locked and the file is tied to the licensed Windows user, so copies on other computers show as unlicensed.
