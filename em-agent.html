Cleanroom EM Trend Agent

Enter recent environmental monitoring results for one location. The agent applies EU GMP Annex 1 action limits for the grade, your alert limit and trend rules, draws the trend and drafts the summary. Sample values are pre-filled (fictitious).

Action limits follow EU GMP Annex 1 (2022) for viable particles. Use the limits and rules in your own site EM SOP. Demonstration only; it does not replace a validated system or QA decision-making. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
