FMEA Risk Assessment Agent

List what can go wrong in a process step, score Severity, Occurrence and Detection from 1 to 10, and get the Risk Priority Number (RPN = S × O × D), the risk ranking, a priority chart and a draft summary. Sample values are pre-filled (fictitious).

#Process stepFailure modeEffectCurrent controlSODRPN

S = how serious the effect is for the patient or product. O = how often it happens. D = how hard it is to detect before release (10 = very hard).

Demonstration only. It supports a risk assessment under ICH Q9 but does not replace your SOP, a validated system or QA approval. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
