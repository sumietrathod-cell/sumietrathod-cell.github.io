Where are the buttons? In every agent, the buttons How to use My company Live data Fill from Excel Download Excel backup sit at the bottom left of the screen.

1Start the free demo

  1. Open any agent from the website, for example the OOS Investigation Agent.
  2. Enter your name and email or WhatsApp number, then tap Start free demo.
  3. All agents in your browser are open for 7 days. The bar at the bottom shows the days left.

Every agent opens with sample data, so you see a full result before typing anything.

2Buy and activate your license

  1. Choose a plan on the pricing page and tap Buy on WhatsApp.
  2. After payment you receive a license key and a one-tap activation link on WhatsApp.
  3. Tap the activation link on each device you use. Or open any agent, tap Enter key (or I already have a license key), paste the key and tap Activate.
  4. Your company name now shows at the bottom right: "Licensed to …". The key works on all agents in your plan.
Keep the WhatsApp message with your key. If you change phone, laptop or browser, open the activation link again.

4Fill from Excel

  1. Tap Fill from Excel, then Download Excel template. Row 1 holds the field names, row 2 shows the sample.
  2. Keep row 1 as it is. Add one row per record, starting from row 2.
  3. Tap Fill from Excel again and upload the file. If it has several rows, tap the row you want.
  4. The form fills and the result appears.

For upload agents, download the template from the upload box where offered (for example OOS bulk check and APQR Pro), or keep the column names shown in the agent's sample table.

5Live data from Google Sheets

Keep your records in your own Google Sheet. The agent reads the newest data every time you open it, so you only maintain the Sheet.

  1. Create a Google Sheet in your own Google account with the same columns as the Excel template.
  2. In Google Sheets: File → Share → Publish to web. Choose the sheet, choose Comma-separated values (.csv), then Publish.
  3. Copy the link. In the agent tap Live data, paste the link and tap Connect and load now.
  4. The button changes to "Live: Google Sheet". From now on the dashboard updates every time you open the agent. Tap Refresh now to update during the day.
Important: anyone who has a "Publish to web" link can read that sheet. Do not share the link, and do not put patient names or confidential batch data in it. To stop, use File → Share → Publish to web → Stop publishing in Google Sheets.

No Google Sheet? The agent remembers the last Excel file you uploaded on that device and shows it again when you return.

6Add your company logo

  1. Tap My company.
  2. Upload your logo (PNG or JPG) and enter company name, address, phone, email and GST or licence number.
  3. Tap Save. Your logo and name now appear at the top of every agent, matching fields (business name, address) fill in, and printed or PDF reports carry your letterhead.

7Read, copy and print results

  • Colours: green = within limits or on target, amber = watch or trend, red = out of limits, overdue or needs action.
  • Copy summary: copies the draft report or email so you can paste it into Word, email or your QMS.
  • Print / save PDF: prints the report with your letterhead. Choose "Save as PDF" in the print window.
  • Download for Word: available in APQR Pro for an editable report.
  • Share on WhatsApp: in business agents such as Salary Slip and Trip Splitter.
Agent outputs are drafts. Check them against your records and have them reviewed and approved as your SOP requires.

8Keep your backup

Your entries stay in your browser and are not sent to us. Tap Download Excel backup to save the entries, tables and results of the current agent as an Excel-readable file on your computer. Do this at the end of each working session or before clearing your browser.

9Devices and browsers

  • Works in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox on phone, tablet and laptop. Use the latest version.
  • License, company details and remembered files are saved per browser. On a new device, open the activation link and add your company details once.
  • Private or incognito windows forget everything when closed.
  • Always open agents from the website link. Saved copies of the page do not receive updates.

10Your data and GxP

  • Agents run in your browser. Aarohan does not receive or store the data you enter.
  • Google Sheets used for Live data stay in your own Google account.
  • Agents are planning, tracking and drafting aids. They are not validated computerised systems under 21 CFR Part 11 or EU GMP Annex 11, and they do not replace your SOPs or GMP records.

11Problems and answers

"This key is not valid"

Copy the whole key including AQA1- at the start, with no spaces. Or simply tap the activation link from WhatsApp.

"This agent is not in your license"

Your plan covers other agents. Tap Upgrade on WhatsApp to add this agent or bundle.

My demo ended

Buy a license on WhatsApp and enter the key. Your browser keeps the agents you used.

Fill from Excel says the column names do not match

Download the template from that agent and copy your data into it without changing row 1.

Live data says the Sheet is not published

In Google Sheets use File → Share → Publish to web, choose CSV and publish. A normal "Share" link is not enough.

My data disappeared

Browser data was cleared or you used another browser. Upload your last Excel backup, or reconnect your Google Sheet with Live data.

Excel button says it needs internet

Excel reading loads once from the internet. Connect and try again; after that it works normally.
