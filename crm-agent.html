CRM Follow-up Agent

The agent reads your leads, decides who needs a follow-up today, ranks them by deal value and how long they have been waiting, and drafts the WhatsApp message. Sample leads (fictitious) are loaded.

CSV columns: name, phone, interest, stage (New/Contacted/Quote sent/Negotiation/Won/Lost), value, last_contact (YYYY-MM-DD)

Demonstration only. Nothing is sent automatically; you tap the WhatsApp button yourself. Data stays in your browser.
